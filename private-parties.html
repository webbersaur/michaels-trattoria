Frequently Asked Questions

How many guests can the private dining rooms accommodate?

Michael's Trattoria has four private dining rooms ranging from 16 to 75 guests. Our Grand Room seats up to 75, the Bar Room accommodates up to 40 with a dedicated bar setup, the Garden Room seats up to 30, and our Intimate Room is perfect for groups of up to 16.

Is the private dining room fully enclosed?

Yes — our private dining room is a dedicated space separate from the main dining area with its own door, giving you and your guests complete privacy for your event.

What types of events can you host?

We host a wide range of private events including birthday celebrations, milestone anniversaries, rehearsal dinners, bridal showers, corporate dinners, business meetings, holiday parties, family gatherings, retirement parties, graduations, and memorial luncheons.

Can I customize the menu for my event?

Absolutely. Our chef works directly with you to create a custom menu tailored to your event and preferences. You can also build your event menu from our dinner menu or choose from our catering menu for platter-style service.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Yes — we're happy to accommodate vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and allergy-specific dietary needs. Just let your event coordinator know during the planning process so we can prepare accordingly.

What bar service options are available?

We offer full bar service with curated selections from our wine list. Options include open bar, per-drink service, and wine by the bottle. Your event coordinator can help you choose the best option for your budget and occasion.

Is there audio-visual equipment available?

Yes — our private dining room includes A/V capabilities for presentations, slideshows, and photo montages. It's a great option for corporate meetings and milestone birthday or anniversary celebrations.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking 2 to 4 weeks in advance, though we can sometimes accommodate shorter notice. Popular dates — especially weekends and holidays — tend to book faster, so earlier is better for those.

Can I bring my own decorations or cake?

Yes — you're welcome to bring your own decorations, flowers, balloons, and cake. Our team can assist with setup before your guests arrive. We also offer a selection of housemade desserts if you'd prefer to leave the sweets to us.

Is there parking available?

Yes — there is free street parking along Center Street and a public municipal parking lot nearby in downtown Wallingford, just steps from our front door.

How do I book a private party?

Give us a call at (203) 269-5303 and ask about private events. Our dedicated event coordinator will walk you through menu options, room setup, timing, and all the details to make your event perfect.

Is the restaurant accessible for guests with disabilities?

Yes — Michael's Trattoria and our private dining room are wheelchair accessible.

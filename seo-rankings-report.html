Optimization Work Log

February 10, 2025 — Initial Assessment & Website Launch

Completed SEO assessment of the newly launched static site (migrated from WordPress):

  • Baseline rankings recorded: #9 for "italian restaurant wallingford ct", not ranking for broad "restaurants" queries.
  • Critical issue identified: Google still indexing old WordPress URLs — new pages not yet discovered.
  • Foundations built: Meta descriptions, Restaurant/Menu JSON-LD schema, canonical URLs, Open Graph tags, XML sitemap, robots.txt, mobile-responsive design, and DoorDash OrderAction schema all implemented.
  • 301 redirects configured: 22 permanent redirects from old WordPress URLs (e.g., /dinner/ → /dinner-menu) via vercel.json to preserve link equity and prevent 404s.
  • Sitemap submitted to Google Search Console to accelerate indexing of new pages.
February 19, 2026 — On-Site SEO Audit & Fixes

Completed comprehensive on-site SEO audit and implemented fixes across all 9 pages:

  • H1 tags fixed: menu.html H1 changed from decorative "Come and Experience" to keyword-rich "OUR MENUS." All 6 menu sub-pages now include brand name in H1 (e.g., "MICHAEL'S TRATTORIA DINNER MENU").
  • Title tags optimized: Shortened 3 over-length titles to under 60 characters for proper SERP display.
  • Meta descriptions trimmed: 5 descriptions shortened to under 160 chars with CTAs added.
  • Page speed improved: Added defer to JavaScript and converted fonts + CSS to non-render-blocking async loading on all 8 sub-pages (matching homepage pattern).
  • Breadcrumb navigation added: Visual breadcrumbs on all sub-pages + BreadcrumbList JSON-LD schema for rich results eligibility.
  • Internal linking strengthened: "Browse Other Menus" cross-link section on all 6 menu pages. Fixed homepage footer menu link. Added catering menu link to private events page.
  • Schema improvements: Added @id to Restaurant entity for connected knowledge graph. Updated all Menu schemas to reference it. Added twitter:card meta tags site-wide.
  • Housekeeping: Updated copyright to 2026. Updated sitemap lastmod dates.
March 3, 2026 — Rankings Re-evaluation

Re-checked all keyword positions against live Google results:

  • "restaurants wallingford ct" — broke into page 1 at #10. Previously not in top 10. Michael's is now one of only 5 individual restaurant sites on page 1 for this broad keyword (alongside Serafino's, Colony Diner, Terrace on the Green, Gaetano's, and Cranky Coqui). Aggregators (TripAdvisor, Yelp, OpenTable) hold 3 of the top 4 spots.
  • New competitor emerging: Luce Osteria Parker Farms (from the Luce Restaurant Group in Hamden) announced a Wallingford location at 295 Parker Farms Rd (former Laskara site). Over 35 years of brand authority will enter the local Italian market.
  • Serafino's remains #1 for Italian keywords and #2 for the broad "restaurants" query — consistently the top organic competitor across all tracked keywords.

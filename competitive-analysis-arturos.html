Why This Matters

A custom-built site gives Michael's full control over page speed, structured data, and technical SEO — none of which can be fully optimized on Wix. Arturo's Wix site injects heavy JavaScript bundles and third-party tracking that drag down Best Practices scores and page load times. Structured data (Schema markup) helps Google understand menus, hours, prices, and FAQs directly — Michael's has comprehensive schema while Arturo's has none.

What This Means for Michael's

Your Competitive Advantages

  • Technical foundation is best-in-class Perfect 100/100/100 Lighthouse scores are rare for any restaurant website. Your custom site is faster, more accessible, and better optimized than virtually all competitors in the CT restaurant space. Maintain this advantage
  • Structured data gives you rich search results Your comprehensive Schema markup (menus with prices, FAQ, breadcrumbs, restaurant details) gives Google everything it needs to display rich results — something Arturo's site provides none of. Maintain this advantage
  • Review volume signals trust and authority 227 Yelp reviews vs 70 is a significant gap. Combined with TripAdvisor and OpenTable presence, Michael's sends stronger trust signals to both Google and potential customers. Keep building reviews

Opportunities to Extend the Lead

  • Target "best italian restaurant CT" and broader regional terms With your strong technical foundation, you're well-positioned to compete beyond Wallingford for regional searches like "best italian restaurant new haven county" or "italian restaurant near me" in the broader CT shoreline area. Medium impact

Our Story

A Tradition of Excellence

Since 1994, Michael's Trattoria has been a cornerstone of Wallingford's dining scene. Founded by Michael Tiscia at 344 Center Street in the heart of downtown Wallingford, the restaurant reflects a lifetime devoted to Italian cuisine — from growing up in his family's restaurant in Stamford, to opening his first restaurant in Norwalk, to training under a Master Chef and serving as head chef at Victoria Station.

More than 30 years later, that same passion drives every dish that leaves our kitchen. From handcrafted pastas and fresh seafood to premium steaks and our legendary Eggplant Parmesan, each plate is prepared with the care and authenticity that Wallingford families have come to trust. Whether it's a weeknight dinner, a special celebration, or a glass of wine at the bar, Michael's Trattoria is where the community gathers.
